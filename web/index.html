What Compass does

  • Compass shows how your week is going in the parts of life you care about.
  • Each day it gives you a short brief: what is furthest behind, one pattern, and one thing to do in 30 minutes or less, with the numbers it used.
  • You log a few numbers a day. Blanks are fine and never count against you.
  • Everything stays on this laptop. Nothing is sent anywhere.
